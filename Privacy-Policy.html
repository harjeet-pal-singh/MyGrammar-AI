2 Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you use MyGrammar AI, we collect only the minimum personal information necessary to provide our services. Authentication is handled through a third-party sign-in provider. When you sign in, we receive your email address and display name solely to identify your account and provide access to the App. We do not store your password or any other sensitive credential.

Usage Information

We may collect limited usage-related information to ensure proper functioning of the App. This data is used only for operational purposes.

Device Information

We may automatically collect basic device-related information such as device type, operating system version, and app version. This information helps us maintain compatibility, improve performance, and fix bugs. We do not use this information to identify you personally.

5 Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. Authentication is delegated to a secure third-party identity provider using industry-standard protocols, and we do not store any passwords on our servers. 6 Data Retention

Saved grammar corrections remain available in your history until you manually remove them, or until account management features (such as account deletion) are introduced in future updates.

Currently, in-app account deletion and data removal features are not yet available, but are planned for a future release.
