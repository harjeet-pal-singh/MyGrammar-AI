Account Deletion Request

My Grammar AI

My Grammar AI uses Google Sign-In to create your account. This page explains how you can request deletion of your account and associated email and profile data using Gmail.

How to request account deletion

Step 1

Open Gmail on your computer or mobile device.

Step 2

Send an email to the address below with the required subject line:

support@mygrammarai.com

Subject line must be: ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST: My Grammar AI

Step 3

Include the following information in your email body:

  • The Gmail address you used to sign in with Google
  • Your name (optional)
  • The sentence: "I request deletion of my My Grammar AI account and associated data"
Step 4

Send the email. Our support team will review your request and respond.

Data that will be deleted

Data type Action
Email address associated with your account Deleted
Profile name Deleted
Profile avatar or photo Deleted

Data that may be retained

Data type Reason
Aggregated, anonymous analytics Internal reporting - no personally identifiable information
Email correspondence about your deletion request For compliance and support records

Important: Deleting your My Grammar AI account does NOT delete your Google account. Your Gmail, Drive, and other Google services remain completely unaffected.

What if I cannot send an email?

If you are unable to send an email, please reinstall the My Grammar AI app from the Google Play Store, log in, and contact support through the in-app help section. Gmail is the preferred method for deletion requests.

Your Google account

My Grammar AI only has access to basic Google profile information (email, name, avatar) via OAuth. We cannot delete your Google account. To delete your Google account entirely, please visit Google Account Settings.

Checklist before you email

  • You are using the Gmail address associated with your My Grammar AI account
  • Subject line says: ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST: My Grammar AI
  • You include your registered email in the body
  • You understand this does not delete your Google account
